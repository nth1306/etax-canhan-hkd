# Vietnam 2026 Personal, HKD & Real Estate Tax Reference (TVPL Verified)

Verified legal citations directly extracted from `thuvienphapluat.vn` for `2026` tax obligations.

## 1. Key Thresholds & Tax Calculation (`2026`)

### 1.1 HKD & Individual Property Rental (`BĐS`)
- **Primary Laws:**
  - **VBHN `112/VBHN-VPQH`** (`20/05/2026` — consolidating **Luật Thuế TNCN `109/2025/QH15`** & **Luật `09/2026/QH16`** effective **`01/01/2026`**)
  - **Luật Thuế GTGT `48/2024/QH15`** (amended by **Điều 2 Luật `09/2026/QH16`** effective **`01/01/2026`**)
  - **NĐ `68/2026/NĐ-CP`** amended by **NĐ `141/2026/NĐ-CP`** (`29/04/2026`, effective **`01/01/2026`**)
  - **NQ `198/2025/QH15`** (**Khoản 7 Điều 10**): abolishes license fee (`lệ phí môn bài`) from **`01/01/2026`**; **Khoản 6 Điều 10**: abolishes presumptive lump-sum tax (`thuế khoán`).
- **Exemption Threshold (`<= 01 tỷ đồng/năm`, Khoản 1 Điều 1 NĐ `141/2026/NĐ-CP`):**
  - `0 đ` VAT (`Điều 3 NĐ 68/2026`), `0 đ` PIT (`Điều 4 NĐ 68/2026`), no mandatory e-invoices (`điểm b Khoản 5 Điều 8 NĐ 68/2026`).
- **When Revenue `> 01 tỷ đồng/năm` (CRITICAL VAT vs PIT difference):**
  - **PIT (`TNCN`):** Calculated **ONLY on the portion EXCEEDING `01 tỷ đồng`** (`Khoản 3 & 4 Điều 7 VBHN 112/VBHN-VPQH`; `NĐ 141/2026` allows deducting the `1 tỷ` exemption against the highest-rate/most favorable line or contract).
  - **VAT (`GTGT`):** Calculated on **TOTAL taxable revenue** (`điểm c Khoản 2 Điều 12 Luật GTGT 48/2024/QH15`).
- **Tax Rates (`%` of revenue):**
  - **Property Rental (`BĐS`, excl. accommodation):** `5%` VAT + `5%` PIT.
  - **Goods distribution / retail:** `1%` VAT + `0.5%` PIT.
  - **Food & Beverage / services with goods:** `3%` VAT + `1.5%` PIT.
  - **Pure services:** `5%` VAT + `2%` PIT.

### 1.2 Salary & Wage PIT (`Tiền lương, tiền công` — `NQ 110/2025/UBTVQH15`, effective `01/01/2026`)
- **Taxpayer deduction (bản thân):** **`15,5 triệu đồng/tháng`** (**`186 triệu đồng/năm`**).
- **Dependent deduction (mỗi người phụ thuộc):** **`6,2 triệu đồng/tháng`**.

## 3. Misallocated Prior-Year Payments & Late-Payment Interest Relief
- **Beyond `Điều 37` Correction Window:** Payment receipts from a prior budget year audited in the following year **cannot** use treasury receipt correction (`Tra soát C1-11/NS` / `Điều 37 TT 89/2026`) after the annual budget settlement cutoff (`Luật NSNN 89/2025/QH15`). Use **`01/ĐNXLNT`** (`điểm c Khoản 2 & c.2 Khoản 5 Điều 42 TT 89/2026`) to **offset across CQTs / provinces / sub-items** (e.g. `857` -> `557` or CQT tỉnh A -> CQT nơi có khoản nợ).
- **Late-Payment Interest Relief (`điểm g Khoản 3 Điều 26 NĐ 252/2026/NĐ-CP` + `Khoản 4 Điều 42 TT 89/2026`):** No late-payment interest (`0.03%/day`) applies to the offset portion from the **date the overpayment occurred** (date paid into NSNN) through the date the tax authority executes the offset.

## 4. Operational Handbook Rules (Sổ tay HKD — Cục Thuế `03/2026`, adjusted for `NĐ 141/2026` `1 tỷ` threshold)

- **Mandatory Revenue Book Even When Tax = `0 đ` (`Trang 21`):** Households/individuals below the exempt threshold (`<= 1 tỷ` post-NĐ `141/2026`) do not need complex accounting vouchers, **but MUST maintain `Sổ doanh thu bán hàng hóa, dịch vụ` (`Mẫu S1a-HKD`, `TT 152/2025/TT-BTC`)** electronically or on paper and **retain for >= 05 years**.
- **Mandatory Bank Account / E-Wallet Notice (`01/BK-STK`, `Trang 22–23`):** Notify CQT of business bank accounts/e-wallets (`01/BK-STK`) initially and whenever changed.
- **Multi-Year Prepaid Rent & Organization Withholding (`Trang 14 & 37–38`):**
  - Multi-year lump-sum rental payments may be **allocated evenly by year** or declared once.
  - When a tenant organization (e.g., a company) withholds & files tax (`01/TCKT` + `02/BK-KTBĐS`), the **lease contract MUST explicitly state** withholding responsibility and how much of the `1 tỷ` threshold exemption is allocated to that contract.
  - Individuals always self-file via **`01/BĐS` + `01/BK-BĐS`**, paying tax by **payment ID** per property location.
- **20% VAT Rate Reduction Exception (`NQ 204/2025/QH15` & `NĐ 174/2025/NĐ-CP`, `Trang 50–52`):** Applies to eligible HKD goods/services from `01/07/2025` through **`31/12/2026`**, **EXCLUDING real estate business/rentals (`kinh doanh BĐS`)** and sugary drinks `>5g/100ml` from `01/01/2026`.
- **Automatic Refund & Lifecycle Forms (`Trang 52–56`):**
  - Reclaim platform over-withholding automatically via refund fields on **`01/TKN-CNKD`** (or **`02/CNKD-TNCN-QTT`**) when data matches tax authority records.
  - Lifecycle: Amend `GCN ĐKHKD` fields (`Tên HKD`, `Ngành nghề chính`, `Trụ sở`, `Vốn`, `Chủ hộ`) via **`https://hokinhdoanh.dkkd.gov.vn`** / ward `UBND` one-stop linkage (`một cửa liên thông ĐKHKD – ĐKT`; DVC rejects direct `08-MST` with error `0525`); use **`08-MST`** (`TT 86/2024/TT-BTC`, within `10` working days) ONLY for non-GCN fields or HKDs without a `GCN ĐKHKD`. **`23/ĐK-TCT`** (temporary suspension without business license), **`24/ĐK-TCT`** (MST termination after clearing all tax debts), **`01/TB-ĐĐKD`** (`điểm đ Khoản 4 Điều 8 NĐ 68/2026/NĐ-CP` — add/modify/suspend/terminate secondary HKD locations). Check for **MST Status `10`** (`chờ cập nhật thông tin số định danh cá nhân`) if CCCD is not yet synced with national population DB.
